# Pastel Board Game Tiles

Terrain art by qubodup, 2019.

Source: https://github.com/qubodup/pastel-tiles

Used under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 license:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

The PNG files in this directory were exported from the author's layered XCF
sources without redrawing the artwork. Their visible layers were flattened and
the sea artwork received the color adjustment from the author's export script.
